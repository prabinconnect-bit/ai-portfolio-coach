What AI Portfolio Coach does

AI Portfolio Coach provides instructional guidance through ChatGPT to help users customize a portfolio website. The plugin package does not include its own database, analytics system, or separate account-registration system.

Information handled by ChatGPT

When you use the coach, information you choose to enter may be processed by ChatGPT and OpenAI's systems to provide a response. ChatGPT's collection, retention, and use of conversation data are governed by OpenAI's own terms, privacy disclosures, and account settings. This website and the plugin creator do not control those platform-level practices. Review OpenAI's current privacy information before using ChatGPT with personal or confidential information.

Separate collection by the creator

The plugin itself is not designed to send user inputs to a separate service or store them in a creator-operated database. If you contact support or submit a public issue, the information you provide may be handled by the service you use (for example, GitHub) and may be visible to others depending on the settings of that service. Do not post passwords, private contact details, or sensitive information in public issues.

Website hosting and logs

This site is intended to be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information under its own policies when visitors access hosted pages. Check GitHub's current privacy documentation for details; the site owner does not operate an independent analytics system as part of this site.

Children

This service is not designed for children under 13, and it is not intended to solicit their personal information.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated if the plugin or its data practices change. For privacy questions, use the support method listed on the Support page.
