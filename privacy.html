Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

1. About AI Portfolio Coach

AI Portfolio Coach provides educational guidance through ChatGPT to help beginners customize a personal portfolio website. The plugin package is not designed to operate its own user database, analytics system, or account-registration system.

2. Information processed by ChatGPT

Information you choose to enter into ChatGPT may be processed by OpenAI and its systems to generate responses. The handling of conversations, account information, retention, and data use is governed by applicable OpenAI terms, privacy disclosures, and your account settings.

AI Portfolio Coach does not control OpenAI's platform-level data practices. Review OpenAI's current privacy information and avoid entering confidential information unless you understand the applicable privacy implications.

3. Collection by the plugin creator

The plugin itself is not designed to send prompts to a separate creator-operated service or store them in a creator-operated database. This describes the intended design of the plugin, not the data practices of ChatGPT or other third-party services.

If you contact support or submit a public issue, information you share may be processed by GitHub or another service. Public issues may be visible to others. Do not include passwords, private account details, or sensitive information.

4. Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with website access under its own policies. This project does not intentionally operate a separate website analytics system.

5. Third-party services

AI Portfolio Coach may link to ChatGPT, Framer, GitHub, and other third-party services. Those providers have their own privacy policies and practices. This policy does not govern information collected directly by those providers.

6. Children's privacy

AI Portfolio Coach is not designed to solicit personal information from children under 13. Please do not submit children's personal information through the service.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the project or its data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about this project, visit the Support page.
